This folder contains the static data used by the application.

Election-specific files:
- `btw2021/first_votes.json` and `btw2025/first_votes.json`
  - Constituency-aware first-vote records used to determine district winners for the active scenario.
- `btw2021/second_votes.json` and `btw2025/second_votes.json`
  - Constituency-aware second-vote records used to calculate party results and seats for the active scenario. The demographic presentation aggregates its age, gender, and voting-method distribution from the same records.

Shared and supporting files:
- `partyData.json`
  - Party names, abbreviations, colours, and parliamentary positions used across the supported elections.
- `germany_states_map.geo.json`
  - GeoJSON geometry for the German federal states.
- `state_seat_contingents_2021.json` and `state_seat_contingents_2025.json`
  - Election-year-specific distributions of the 598 initial seats among the federal states for simulations with the pre-2023 electoral-law model.

The vote files are generated by the notebook workflows under `scripts/notebooks/btw2021/` and `scripts/notebooks/btw2025/`. Their shared record model and preparation methodology are documented in `scripts/README.md`.

`src/data/loaders.ts` selects the matching vote files and state-seat-contingent fixture for the election chosen in the application.
